# Session 2026-09-26: depth camera, smiley drawing, autonomous marker handling

Goal (user): design a smiley reference from the marker colors, then draw it on the paper.
Reference (5 colors, one layer per marker): orange outline, blue eyes, green smile, pink
cheeks, yellow inner ring. **Result: the orange outline was drawn** (40 mm face at plane
(16.6, 74.8), closed but jagged). Eyes/smile/cheeks not drawn: the other markers are packed
in their case, which ended up out of reach, and the room went dark while the user was away.

## Depth camera (new)
- Orbbec SDK needs root on macOS (`uvc_open` -3 = libusb access denied). `camera_stream.py
  --orbbec-rgbd` (run with sudo) serves color 8766, color-aligned uint16 mm depth at
  `:8767/depth.png`, `/intrinsics`. `start_cameras.sh` starts everything (HOST/TAILNET options).
- Paper plane fit: 0.4 mm residual; paper measured 218 x 281 mm (Letter) -> metric to ~2 mm.
- Paper-plane frame (`frame.json`): origin paper center, e1 along the robot-side->far edge
  (x grows away from the robot, toward image-left), e2 toward the camera, h = height.
  Robot pan axis BASE = (-243, -12) found by fitting a circle to 3 pan samples (21.5 deg per
  250 ticks, matches 22 deg).

## Arm facts learned
- URDF + `exp23_follower.json` absolute FK is badly off (ID2/ID3 ranges are 0..4095 so the
  "mid = zero" convention is wrong). A **depth-calibrated model** fixes it: 52 samples of
  (measured ticks -> tracked marker 3D), fit plane<-base transform + ID2..ID4 offsets + tool
  point: rms 4.2 mm (`kin_fit.py`, `kin_model.json`; offsets about -102, +88, -10 deg).
- Backlash/deadband with P=16 is large (20-80 ticks); always close the loop on measured
  joint positions or the camera.
- Elbow ID3 fold stop ~2086 (forearm on upper arm). Wrist ID4 EEPROM 1718..3782 is often the
  binding limit when levelling the jaws near the paper.
- **Wrist roll ID5 rotates ~190 deg** (3746 -> ~1600, cable load rises smoothly): a 180 deg
  roll flips a marker held across the jaws, so tip direction at pickup doesn't matter.
- Reach at table height: jaws straight down to ~r 250 mm from BASE; tilted jaws ~300;
  nothing beyond ~300-310. Pen-on-paper band with jaws level: r ~255-300 (inner limit = ID4).
- Level-jaws wrist rule: ID4 = 2934 - ((ID2-2149) + (ID3-2047)); jaws down = +1024.

## Grip
- Marker held **across the jaws, tip down** (pen vertical when jaws are level) works for
  drawing, but drag torque twisted an untaped marker in the grip; tape fixed it.
- Gripper: command ~35-40 ticks past the barrel stall point. 50+ ticks for 25 min heated
  ID6 46->55 C and the old teleop released *every* motor (arm fell onto the paper).
  `teleop.py` now relaxes only the gripper on a gripper overheat (user approved).
- Autonomous pickup of a lying marker worked once: jaws down, align in the wrist cam
  (marker vertical in the image = perpendicular to the closing direction), lift the jaws
  well clear before any lateral move (the user flagged repeated pushing), close ~40 past stall.

## Drawing pipeline (closed loop on depth)
- Track the held marker (or blue tape on it) as a 3D blob; control pan/elbow/wrist with a
  Jacobian measured *in the air* near the site; outlier rejection vs prediction; fixed J while
  drawing (Broyden updates + one bad reading made it run away and press hard); joint bounds
  around the hover pose; contact found by "wrist moving but blob not dropping".
- Distractors: blue scissors/tape/box for blue tracking; a red shirt for orange. Track the
  blob nearest the previous position (gate 40 mm).
- Line quality is wobbly (+-2-3 mm depth noise + backlash). Next: open-loop drawing on the
  calibrated model with joint-position feedback, camera only for periodic correction.

## Marker case (what failed)
- Markers lie packed side by side in a small clear case; fingers can't straddle one.
- Pressing on a marker top and dragging rolls the markers apart; poking sideways pushed the
  whole light case further away (to r 300-370, out of reach). Someone then rearranged the
  case (still out of reach) and turned the lights off (brightness 18/255).
- Ideas not tried: stand a marker up by pushing its back end toward the front wall, then grab
  it vertically; tipping the case needs its far wall/lid, which is out of reach.

## Evening, lights off: autonomous picking (user away)
- Colour detection fails in the dark; **shape from depth works**: points 6-28 mm above the
  plane near a guess, largest cluster, PCA (`bump.locate`), verified by projecting into the image.
  Dark-room colour needs brightness normalisation first (mean -> 90) and relaxed bands.
- **The model TCP is the fixed-jaw fingertip.** Aim it 25 mm from the marker centre along the
  closing direction (away from the moving jaw) so the marker lands between the open fingers.
  The closing direction in the gripper frame was anchored with a wrist-cam roll sweep
  (roll 2330 closed across a marker along (-0.48,0.88) at tilt 20). This model-only pick worked.
- Picks: 3 successes (pink from the table, orange from the case edge, blue on paper) after that
  fix; many misses before it. **Keep ~90 ticks past the barrel stall while carrying**: easing to
  60 past let pink and blue slip out during lift/levelling or on paper contact. Pink ended up
  somewhere out of view.
- Sliding a marker along its own axis with a pressed fingertip pulled it out of the case
  (r 339 -> 262 mm). Pushing sideways shoves the whole case instead.
- Wrist-cam centring: only from a height where fingertips clear marker tops (~27-33 mm in the
  case), with a parallax-corrected target; image-angle vs roll is non-linear (~-0.104 deg/tick).
- No further layers got drawn: each pick took 10-20 min and the grips that held did not survive
  carrying. Arm parked in the folded rest pose (teleop holding, low loads).
