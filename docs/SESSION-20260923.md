## Controller
`teleop.py` holds all six motors and reads JSON commands appended to a file
(`joints`, `delta`, `gripper`, `speed`, `release`); `teleop_cmd.sh` appends a command,
waits for motion to finish, prints status and saves both camera frames (edit its
scratch path). Servo status flags (e.g. voltage warning) are logged, not fatal;
communication errors leave torque on (holding). Log: `captures/teleop-20260923.log`.

## Learned mappings (raw ticks, this calibration)
- Gripper (ID 6): open = higher; fully closed ~690, fully open ~1030 (stalls).
  EEPROM limits widened to 0..4095 (old 1679..3130); readings no longer match
  `exp23_follower.json`.
- Pan ~1200 faces the Gemini tripod, ~2700 faces the far side of the desk.
- Low P gain (16): shoulder/elbow stop 20-100 ticks short under gravity; overshoot targets.
- URDF FK height estimates were off by several cm; steer visually.
- Flat blades slip under the jaw tips. What worked: wrist roll ~3860 (jaws close
  parallel to desk), a handle loop framed between the open jaws, tips at desk level, close.
- Monocular Gemini depth is misleading from its low angle; a 1080p still
  (stop the stream first) resolves jaw/object contact much better.

## Known issues
- `arm_session.py park` can stall: +/-24-tick steps don't overcome gravity with P=16,
  so it never gets within 16 ticks; an interrupt then leaves torque on.
- `camera_stream.py` can orphan its ffmpeg child when killed; check `ps` for ffmpeg.
